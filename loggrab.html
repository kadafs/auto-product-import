QGA25 Log Grab

$500.00 – $2,650.00Price range: $500.00 through $2,650.00

The QGA25 Log Grab is designed to safely handle logs by tractor, skid steer, telehandler or forklift.  Its heavy duty construction includes two fixed lower arms and hinged upper arms activated by two double acting hydraulic cylinders. The upper arms design allows sorting of logs.

FEATURES:

  • Used to safely handle logs, stumps or bushes
  • Suitable for fitment to a tractor, skid steer, telehandler or forklift
  • Strong construction and double acting hydraulic cylinders ensure a firm grip on different timber diameters
  • Curved upper arm and rounded back allows logs to be easily rolled into grapple, ideal when bundling logs
  • Excellent operator visibility
  • One hydraulic function required
  • Individually designed to suit quick hitch or Forklift Class 2/3 carriage on request
  • Also available as a slip-on C2 forklift attachment  on request
  • Ideal for use in forestry and agriculture
  • Enamel paint finish
